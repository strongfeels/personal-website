clonskeagh

Sixteen square kilometres of south Dublin rebuilt from real map data and made walkable and drivable in the browser. OpenStreetMap gives footprints and little else, so roof ridges come from a minimum-area rectangle fitted to each building and front doors from the nearest road; trees, hedges and a church's copper domes are measured off satellite imagery. Traffic drives on the left, pedestrians keep to the footpaths, and the Luas runs its true alignment. Geometry streams in chunks, so load time doesn't grow with the map.

Peer-to-peer video app for practising public speaking with strangers. No accounts, no sign-up — a speaker queue rotates through whoever is waiting. Real-time competitive coding platform. Two to four players join a room by code and race head-to-head to solve programming problems, with opponents' progress streaming live over WebSocket, ELO ratings and a global leaderboard. Rust backend on axum and tokio, and a sandboxed executor that runs untrusted submissions with no network access.
